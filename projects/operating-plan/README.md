# Transportation operating plan

Synthetic independent portfolio case by Quincy Jones, with AI-assisted construction and review. All recommendations, owner roles and monitoring observations are simulated; no employer outcomes are claimed.

## Decision

Approve a conditional operating plan and monthly reforecast process. Review volume, price realization and cost inflation together before changing service capacity or contract pricing.

The base case produces $11.31M revenue and $124,447 EBITDA (1.1%). Its December cash is $282,038, only $32,038 above the assumed floor. The downside produces a $616,492 EBITDA loss and a $145,022 negative ending cash balance. The upside is a sensitivity, not committed guidance.

## Alternatives and conditions

A blanket price increase may reduce volume or retention. Capacity reductions may worsen service. The model does not estimate customer elasticity, contract rights or staffing feasibility. Validate these before committing.

Confirm segment definitions, price realization, cost allocations and cash timing. Establish owners for each variance. Preserve the synthetic H1 source records while updating forecast inputs.

## Method and limitations

The model preserves 18 H1 segment records and builds H2 units, price, unit cost, fixed cost and opex. Monthly operating rates compound. A July quantity-price-cost bridge reconciles EBITDA to an explicit reference plan. Cash derives from EBIT, cash taxes, noncash depreciation, receivables/payables changes and capex.

Cash uses a 30-day month convention and flat 35-day receivables / 20-day variable-cost payables. Taxes apply only to positive monthly EBIT. No loss carryforwards, borrowing, interest, dividends or full balance sheet are modeled. The cash schedule begins in July; H1 cash is unavailable.
