# Transportation operating plan

Synthetic independent portfolio analysis with AI-assisted construction and review. Proposed company owners are simulation roles; no employer outcomes are claimed.

## Decision

Approve the base plan conditionally; establish a downside funding response and monthly reforecast before committing spending.

The base full-year 2026 operating view (synthetic H1 actuals plus H2 forecast) produces $11,308,100 revenue, $124,447 EBITDA (1.10% margin) and $508,855 ending cash. Minimum monthly cash is $279,760. Closing debt is $540,000. All six monthly balance sheets reconcile within the tested tolerance.

## Conditions and tradeoffs

Validate opening cash and AR/AP, debt, tax-loss usability, collection timing and capex. Escalate the $264,908 downside shortfall against the $250,000 proposed floor.

A blanket price increase may reduce volume or retention. Capacity reductions may worsen service. The model does not estimate customer elasticity, contract rights or staffing feasibility. Validate these before committing. Opening balances and explicit collection lags change the cash result relative to the earlier simplified model.

## Method and limits

Preserved 18 H1 synthetic segment records feed the operating history. H2 forecasting links units, pricing and costs to collection/payment schedules, PP&E/depreciation, debt/interest, cash taxes, income, cash flow and monthly balance sheets. Full-year revenue and EBITDA include H1; cash and balance-sheet forecasts cover July–December.

no inventory, deferred tax accounting, dividends or new borrowing. Opening tax losses are zero and future losses are assumed fully usable. Collection patterns and asset lives require real-source validation. Earlier operating cash results are superseded because the working-capital and opening-balance scope changed.
