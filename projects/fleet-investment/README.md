# Fleet investment committee

Synthetic independent portfolio analysis with AI-assisted construction and review. Proposed company owners are simulation roles; no employer outcomes are claimed.

## Decision

Prefer leasing under the base assumptions, subject to comparable quotes, mileage and tax usability validation. Its $5,918 advantage over replacement is too narrow for an unconditional commitment.

Base after-tax PV cost is $1,674,901 to keep, $1,447,379 to replace and $1,441,461 to lease. Replacement requires $685,500 initial unlevered cash, within the synthetic $700,000 budget. Lease savings versus keep are $233,440; replacement savings are $227,522.

## Conditions and tradeoffs

Obtain matched service-capacity quotes, validate lease mileage, full immediate tax usability, residual values and borrowing terms. The loan is hypothetical and the lease roll-forward is not full accounting-standard implementation.

Replacement consumes upfront cash and bears residual-value risk. Leasing preserves liquidity but adds contractual payments, usage limits and vendor dependency. Keeping the fleet avoids purchase cash but exposes higher modeled maintenance and downtime.

## Method and limits

Five-year after-tax unlevered cash flows include disposal taxes, new-asset tax depreciation, transition costs, mileage overages and terminal proceeds. A separate purchase loan and simplified lease obligation roll-forward keep financing distinct from unlevered NPV.
