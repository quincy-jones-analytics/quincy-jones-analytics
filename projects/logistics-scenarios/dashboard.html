Independent portfolio demonstration. Shipment costs, ocean economics, air operating costs and vendor ratings are synthetic. Public prices and aircraft specifications are labeled separately. Outputs are modeled, not realized savings.

Fuel sensitivity and lane profitability

Same 1,200 synthetic shipment values as the authoritative transportation case. Implied gallons = synthetic fuel dollars / $6.526 Midwest reference. This normalization preserves the original base case; it is not historical fuel consumption. Region selection is a counterfactual sourcing choice, including for the Toronto lane.

Fuel comparison at unchanged revenue and volume

Contribution = revenue − direct cost. Break-even rate uses loaded miles only; the original dataset excludes empty miles, overhead and corporate allocations. It is not a commercial quote. Service reliability stays unchanged by this cost-only scenario.

Ocean freight and inventory delay

Fictional shipment economics, separate from the trucking case. Annual carry rate includes financing and inventory costs; baseline transit carry is included once. Extra days add incremental carry, avoiding double counting.

Carry cost = inventory value × annual carry rate × transit days / 365. No demand elasticity, lost sales, taxes, demurrage or service penalty is assumed; add evidence before using commercially.

Air-cargo mission economics

Compare two modeled missions at the same demand and distance. Published payload and range are screening ceilings; they do not establish a payload-range curve, net capacity after ULDs, dispatch feasibility or actual trip fuel.

A350F was in flight testing at the source date. The selected fuel reduction is hypothetical, not a measured A350F-versus-777F result. Both operating-cost inputs require airline mission data before drawing a fleet investment conclusion.

Cost/tonne-mile = modeled trip cost / (carried metric tonnes × distance km / 1.609344). Fuel intensity = kg fuel / tonne-km. Utilization means payload utilization, not aircraft hours. Cost/tonne-mile is withheld if demand or distance exceeds the published ceiling. The reference uses Boeing's dated 2024 revenue-payload release, not its current gross structural-payload definition.

Vendor risk review

Fictional vendors. Score each factor 0–100, with higher meaning greater risk. Ownership evaluates change-of-control clarity and governance evidence, not nationality. Scores are a transparent prioritization rubric, not probability of failure.

Factor weights (must total 100%)

Risk = weighted factor scores. 70+ triggers priority review; 40–69.99 review; below 40 monitor. Any missing factor keeps the score blank. Bounds assign 0 and 100 to missing factors to show uncertainty. These are illustrative thresholds; change them in assumptions.json and rebuild for another policy.

Evidence to collect: beneficial ownership and change-of-control terms; data permissions and retention; security control evidence and incident response; lane volume shares; substitutability, tested recovery and exit plans.
