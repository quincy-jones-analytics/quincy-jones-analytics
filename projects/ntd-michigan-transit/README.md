# Michigan Transit Cost and Productivity Benchmark

A public-data decision case built from FTA National Transit Database (NTD) TS2.1 2024 Service Data and Operating Expenses Time Series by Mode.

## Decision question
Which Michigan directly operated motor-bus systems warrant a closer cost and productivity review?

## Finding
Across 15 active Michigan Full Reporters, AAATA's 2024 operating cost per passenger trip is **$8.28**, versus an **$8.53** peer median. AAATA records **16.8 passenger trips per revenue hour**, above the **15.9** peer median. Its 2019–2024 ridership recovery is **75%**, compared with **64%** for the peer median.

The recommendation is to use this as a screen for follow-up: review demand, productivity and controllable operating costs before changing service. NTD agency-level data cannot identify a route that loses money; route-level passenger counts, schedules, fares and cost allocation are required.

## Method and scope
Scope: active Michigan Full Reporters, Motor Bus, Directly Operated. Cost per trip is operating expense divided by unlinked passenger trips; service productivity is unlinked passenger trips per revenue vehicle hour. Peer medians are calculated across the scoped agencies. Values are rounded for presentation.

## Source
Federal Transit Administration, National Transit Database, [2024 TS2.1 files](https://www.transit.dot.gov/ntd/data-product/2024-annual-database-agency-information).
