# SaaS Unit Economics and Pricing

## Question
How should Salesforce improve monetization without relying on a blanket list-price increase?

## Data
Latest fiscal-year Forms 10-K for Salesforce (FY2026), ServiceNow (FY2025), and Adobe (FY2025).

## Method
The Excel model calculates revenue growth, gross margin, simple free cash flow margin, and Rule of 40. Simple FCF is GAAP operating cash flow less purchases of property and equipment.

## Result
Salesforce: 44.3% Rule of 40; ServiceNow: 55.3%; Adobe: 52.0%.

## Recommendation
Test a three-tier Agentforce package: pooled usage in the core platform, committed consumption bands with rollover, and an enterprise outcome bundle combining Agentforce, Data 360, and governance.

## Caveats
Fiscal periods and product mixes differ. Rule of 40 is not a GAAP metric. The pricing scenario is illustrative and needs account-level cohort, usage, retention, and support-cost data.
